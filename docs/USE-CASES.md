# Use cases and E2E foundation

Use cases describe user-visible behavior, not test implementation. Each scenario links stable requirements and names the browser/device scope and evidence. Unit and adapter details stay in tests. Passing mocks or desktop emulation is not physical-device or cloud proof.

## Coverage contract

For a changed journey, cover the applicable success, invalid input, failure/retry, persistence/reload, Back/Forward, late response, account switch/isolation, keyboard, touch, and reduced-motion variants. Preserve the originating query/filter/scroll/focus when the scenario requires it. Record intentional skips and keep live authentication/network traces private.

## Planned scale and data scenarios

UC-SCALE-ACCESS (SCALE-01–04) requires server-enforced bounded pages after whole-result filtering/sorting with deterministic cursors, aggregate/existence queries, bounded DOM/cache, and explicit paged export. Every collection consumer—including Home, ownership badges, card details, tags/decks, pending imports, and mutation refresh—must stop depending on a whole-list load.

UC-SCALE-ACCEPTANCE (SCALE-05) uses isolated synthetic inventories up to 30,000 cards. Measure backend records read, latency growth, browser memory, DOM size, and cache bounds separately. Verify stable ties plus concurrent insert/edit/delete, account change, reload, navigation, and failure/retry. Returning a small frontend slice after a full read fails acceptance.

UC-DATA-CATALOGUE (DATA-01/04) enriches only the bounded public result page with exact-printing private annotations. UC-DATA-PRIVATE (DATA-02–04) queries indexed private membership before hydrating public metadata. UC-DATA-PROGRESSIVE (DATA-05–07) renders bounded public results while owner enrichment is explicitly loading, then reconciles in place without disturbing focus, hover, zoom, drag, order, or newer mutations.

## Manual and physical acceptance

Real-phone acceptance records device/browser, conditions, duration, input method, network, and whether results came from a physical camera or synthetic/recorded media. The owner performs physical checks; automation may prepare the protocol and analyze supplied evidence but cannot manufacture heat, battery, audio, camera, or gesture conclusions.

Current physical evidence is limited to the owner-accepted SCAN-10 sequence behavior. SCAN-11/14 long-session thermal/resource behavior, split-card recognition, and broad touch/visual acceptance remain open.
