## Scanner corrections — in progress

Duplicate capture (SCAN-03/04) has priority over split-card handling and the deployment-speed follow-up. No existing draft or owner data should be changed automatically.

- **SCAN-01:** Treat a single physical split card (for example Order // Chaos) as one card even when its printed panels resemble separate cards. Count physical card boundaries rather than printed panels. Accept supported rotations and resolve the combined printing identity as one capture/copy.
- **SCAN-02:** Preserve rejection of two distinct or overlapping physical cards. Do not fix split-card rejection by globally disabling or weakening the one-card gate. Verify split cards, ordinary cards, two separate cards and overlaps with regression fixtures. Distinguish screenshot replay from physical-camera evidence.
- **SCAN-03:** A physical card remaining in the capture area must produce at most one queued copy and one success cue. Glare, foil reflections, autofocus/exposure changes, small movement, transient detection loss and late parallel recognizer responses must not rearm capture. Investigate the reported repeated Elsha, Threefold Master entries; the screenshot alone does not establish the cause.
- **SCAN-04:** Rearm only on validated physical departure/replacement, independently of recognized name. A subsequent physical copy of the same identity must remain scannable. Test stationary reflective cards, transient occlusion/detection loss, actual removal/re-entry, identical consecutive copies and out-of-order recognizer results. Do not silently delete existing draft rows or deduplicate by card name; preserve user review and ownership confirmation.
- **SCAN-05:** Remove the user-facing 50-card session stop. Separate continuous scanning from bounded storage/commit chunks, retaining explicit ownership review, resumable drafts, bounded in-memory work, retry idempotency and visible persistence errors. Verify scanning beyond 50 cards without loss, duplicate commits or forced Review. This is a requested workflow improvement, not an assertion that backend limits can simply be removed.

- **SCAN-06:** Replace routine textual recognition-stage/status overlays with spatial visual feedback: colored translucent masks, outlines and transitions highlighting the relevant card regions. Distinguish framing, processing and result states without obscuring the artwork. Do not invent region localization or processing stages; use actual available geometry and events. Retain accessible status equivalents and concise actionable permission/network/error messages where visual effects alone cannot explain recovery. Respect reduced motion and do not rely on color alone.
- **SCAN-07:** Beside each queued card name, show compact icons for the actual recognition sources that have returned evidence for that capture. Map icons to real configured providers rather than guessed architecture names; provide accessible labels/tooltips. Results arriving after the camera has moved to another card update their original row by capture ID, never the current card. No extra copy or success cue from a late source result; never overwrite user-reviewed choices. Distinguish returned alternatives/failure from corroboration rather than implying every response agrees. Test out-of-order replies, navigation/reload as applicable, and scanning the next card while earlier evidence arrives.

Opening or adding an already-listed printing moves its existing entry to the front rather than duplicating it. Retain exact-printing identity; this history does not change owned quantities, source provenance or pending-import status. Failed/cancelled additions do not count as additions.
- **RECENT-03:** Keep history bounded, persistent across reload and isolated by account.
